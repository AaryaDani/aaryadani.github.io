This summer, I supported the construction of a future Oral Solid Dose (OSD) manufacturing facility through equipment selection, vendor engagement, facility layout development, and operational planning. In addition, I supported a shell freezer replacement project by assisting with root cause investigations, documenting 30+ remediation activities, and supporting hazardous area compliance efforts. These experiences provided invaluable insight into the technical, operational, and collaborative efforts required to deliver pharmaceutical manufacturing facilities.

Beyond the technical experience, I had the opportunity to attend NSBE's E.L.I.T.E. Symposium, where I presented my work on Project Fastnet and connected with senior leadership across the company. I also participated in the University of Pittsburgh's "Shadow in Your City" panel, discussing how academic coursework translates to real-world engineering challenges in industry. These experiences reinforced the importance of communication, relationship-building, and leadership alongside technical expertise.

I am incredibly grateful to Rachelle van den Brand and Jessica Ruderman for the opportunity to work with GES Process. In addition, I would like to thank Matthew O'Donovan, Mark Mathis, and the entire Fastnet DP Team for their mentorship, support, and willingness to share their expertise. I would also like to thank my mentors Gabriela Marziano, Sydney Juda, Hannah Ni, and Cole Moros for their guidance and investment in my growth throughout the summer.
